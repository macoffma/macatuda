# Round 5 — Three concepts beyond fishing

*Note: some players are squeamish about catching/handling fish. Good news — the
Overboard **engine is theme-agnostic**. The whole loop reskins with new nouns and
art; the code and the feel are unchanged. This doc keeps the exact mechanics and
swaps the world.*

## The engine we're reskinning (unchanged in all three)

1. **Go somewhere with hidden richness** — pick a spot; a rumor/dots hint at what's
   there, but you don't know for sure until you work it.
2. **Gather with a press-your-luck "one more"** — keep going for a bigger haul, or
   bank it. Push too far and you **bust**, losing your best find (the fish-snap).
3. **Bring it to market** — a daily **"what's wanted"** pays double until demand's
   full; race rivals to cash in (the Catch-of-the-Day).
4. **Buy fun gear** — three light upgrades (a "see the spots," a "push luck safer,"
   a "carry more").
5. **Mind the commons** — over-harvest and the shared resource collapses, capping
   everyone. Steady play keeps it alive.
6. Most coins after N days wins. AI rivals with personalities stir the pot.

Everything below plugs straight into that.

---

## A. *"Windfall"* — woodland foraging
**Cozy-rustic, warm · plants only, no animals**

- **The spot:** roam glades and thickets; a rumor tells you if a patch looks ripe.
- **Gather verb:** pick mushrooms, berries, and truffles. **Press-luck:** reach
  deeper into the bramble for one more prize — push too far and the light fades /
  the thicket tangles you and you **drop your best basket-find**.
- **The commons:** over-pick a glade and it won't fruit again; if the whole
  village strips the woods, they go barren. (Natural regrowth = a clean commons,
  just like overfishing.)
- **Wanted today:** "The inn wants chanterelles" (pays double).
- **Gear:** Bigger Basket (carry more) · Lantern (forage later & safer — bust
  less) · Forager's Map (see the patches).
- **Casual vs. expert:** casual fills a basket; expert reads which glades are
  ripe, chases the day's wanted find, and forages sustainably.
- *Tone dial:* rustic and mysterious, not saccharine — think misty woods and a
  village market, not googly-eyed mushrooms.

## B. *"Paydirt"* — a gold-rush claim
**Gritty and funny, older-skewing · no living things at all**

- **The spot:** stake a claim along the creek or up a gulch; a rumor hints how
  rich it looks.
- **Gather verb:** pan and dig. **Press-luck:** dig deeper chasing the mother
  lode — push too far and a **cave-in buries your best nugget** and ends your dig.
- **The commons:** the diggings play out; strip the land greedily and the whole
  **boomtown busts early** — nobody wins a ghost town. Fresh strikes each day keep
  it going and mix up where the value is.
- **Wanted today:** "The assay office is paying top dollar for silver."
- **Gear:** Sturdy Pan (bust less) · Mule Cart (haul more) · Claim Map (see the
  ground).
- **Casual vs. expert:** casual pans for whatever glitters; expert stakes the
  right claim, chases the day's metal, and doesn't bust the camp.
- *Tone dial:* saloon characters, tall tales, a little slapstick.

## C. *"Stardust"* — asteroid prospecting
**Cool sci-fi frontier · no living things at all · a nod to M.U.L.E.'s space roots**

- **The spot:** fly to an asteroid; your scanner gives a rough read of what's
  inside.
- **Gather verb:** drill. **Press-luck:** bore toward the rich core — push too far
  and the **rock fractures, you lose your best ore** and scramble back to the ship.
- **The commons:** the belt gets mined out; over-strip it and the field's spent.
  New rocks (and comets) drift in each day — renewal plus fresh variety.
- **Wanted today:** "The station foundry wants platinum."
- **Gear:** Bigger Cargo Hold (carry more) · Reinforced Drill (bust less) ·
  Long-Range Scanner (see the rocks).
- **Casual vs. expert:** casual mines whatever's handy; expert scans for rich
  cores, chases the wanted ore, and works the belt so it lasts. Robot/AI rivals
  fit the setting perfectly.
- *Tone dial:* adventurous and a bit retro-future, great for older kids.

## Squeamish-safety, ranked

## A couple of quick alternates (if none land)

- **"Scrapyard"** — pick through junk heaps for treasure; press-luck rummaging
  risks the pile collapsing. Wholesome "cleaning up" angle, robot rivals.
- **"Orchard"** — shake trees at harvest; crops regrow seasonally (natural
  commons). Warm and agricultural.
