# Overboard — Kitchen-Table Playtest Kit (v0.1)

*The point of this kit: prove the fun on cardboard before building anything.
It tests the one thing that can't be theorized — does a real economy, hidden
inside "catch fish / sell fish," actually feel seamless and create the
low-floor / high-ceiling magic? No screens. ~30 min to set up, ~30 min to play.*

> **Rule of prototyping:** test the smallest thing that could be the whole game.
> Play the **Core Game** below first. Only add the **Layer 2** modules once the
> core feels good. If the core is boring, no amount of upgrades saves it — and
> you'll have learned that for the price of an afternoon, not a codebase.

---

## What we're trying to learn (the hypotheses)

Watch for these while you play — they're the whole reason we're doing this:

1. **Invisible economy.** Do players start *holding* fish instead of dumping, or
   avoiding the crowded spot — *without being told "manage the market"?* If yes,
   the diegesis works: they're playing an economy and don't know it.
2. **The timing leveler.** Does "one more cast?" create groans and laughs — and
   occasionally let the 10-year-old beat the grown-up?
3. **Commons tension.** Does overfishing actually scare people? Do they start
   self-policing, or race to grab before the other guy strips it?
4. **Floor *and* ceiling.** Can the youngest play "catch fish, sell fish" and stay
   in it, while an adult finds the deeper line (cornering the reef, timing sales)?

**Kill criteria (be honest):** if the market feels like fiddly accounting, if the
timing feels random rather than skillful, or if nobody cares about the commons —
the concept needs rework, and that's a *win* to learn now.

## Shopping list (household substitutes)

| Need | Use |
|---|---|
| **3 fish species** (tokens) | 3 colors of something: dried beans, coins, Lego, M&Ms, poker chips. ~30 each. |
| **"Snag" tokens** | A 4th color, ~15. Or torn-paper scraps. |
| **6 fishing spots** | 6 opaque cups / mugs / small paper bags (you draw blind from these). |
| **The bay map** | One sheet of paper: draw 6 circles labeled with the spot names, cups sit on them. |
| **Money** | Poker chips, coins, or scribbled paper cash. |
| **Boats** | A pawn each — bottle caps, meeples, coins with initials. |
| **3 price tracks** | Draw three 1–10 number lines on paper; a coin marks the current price. |

**Species & baseline dock price:**
- 🐟 **Cod** — the staple. **Always sells for 1.** Never moves. (This is the floor:
  a casual can always make steady Cod money and stay in the game.)
- 🐠 **Bass** — mid. Baseline price **3**.
- 🐋 **Tuna** — luxury + the chokepoint. Baseline price **8**. Only lives in two spots.

## Setup (3–4 players)

**Stock each spot's cup** (this is that spot's fish population — kept hidden, you
draw blind):

| Spot | Cod | Bass | Tuna | Snags |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shallows A | 4 | 1 | 0 | 2 |
| Shallows B | 4 | 1 | 0 | 2 |
| Banks A | 5 | 3 | 0 | 2 |
| Banks B | 5 | 3 | 0 | 2 |
| Deep Reef | 4 | 4 | 2 | 3 |
| **The Trench** (chokepoint) | 2 | 3 | 5 | 3 |

**Each player:** a boat, **hold limit 5** fish, and **5 cash**.
**Price markers:** Cod at 1 (it won't move), Bass at 3, Tuna at 8.
**Game length:** **5 Days.** Most cash at the end wins — *if the sea survives* (see
Sea Health).

---

## A Day, phase by phase (the Core Game)

### 1. Forecast *(lagged info — the foresight skill)*
Flip the top **Forecast card** face-up for all to see. It hints at *tomorrow*.
Make a small deck (index cards), shuffle, one per day:
- *"Cannery order: Bass +2 tomorrow"* (Bass baseline up next day)
- *"Sushi boat docks: Tuna +2 tomorrow"*
- *"Storm at the Shallows"* (those two spots closed tomorrow)
- *"Fish run at the Reef"* (add 3 Bass to Deep Reef now)
- *"Glut: everyone caught cod"* (no effect — a fake-out)
- *"Inspector"* (the current cash leader skips their first sale tomorrow — rubber-band)

### 2. Set Sail *(the plot-pick timing leveler)*
Everyone **secretly** picks a spot (write it on a slip, or hold your boat in a
fist), then **reveal at once.**
- **Crowding rule:** if 2+ boats land on the same spot, they *alternate* drawing
  from that one cup — it empties twice as fast and you each get less. The "argh,
  we both picked the Reef!" moment.

### 3. Cast & Reel *(press-your-luck timing — "one more cast?")*
At your spot, draw tokens **one at a time** from the cup into your hold (up to 5):
- Draw a **fish** → it goes in your hold. Draw a **Snag** → return it to the cup
  and **+1 tension** for you this turn.
- After any draw you may **Stop** and bank your hold. Or press on.
- At **tension 2**, one more Snag (**tension 3**) = **LINE SNAPS**: throw your
  single most valuable fish back and your turn ends immediately.
- Sharing a spot? You each track your own tension; you're racing to grab the good
  fish before the cup thins *and* before your line snaps.

*This is the heart of the "seamless" test: you're deciding blind (how rich is this
spot?) and pressing luck (one more?) — and that decision is secretly your supply
quantity.*

### 4. Market *(the elastic economy — hidden in plain sight)*
Bring your hold to the dock and sell, **one player at a time** (rotate who sells
first each day — selling order matters):
- **Cod:** always 1 each. Flat.
- **Bass:** sells at the current price; **each Bass sold (by anyone) drops the
  Bass price by 1** (min 1).
- **Tuna:** sells at current price; **each Tuna sold drops the Tuna price by 2**
  (min 3).

You feel the flood immediately: dump six Bass and you watch the last ones sell for
scraps. **You may hold fish** and sell later — but see spoilage.

### 5. Overnight *(regeneration, spoilage, and the commons)*
- **Spoilage:** any fish you held and *didn't* sell — discard 1 of each species
  (don't hoard forever; forces the timing decision).
- **Prices recover:** Bass +2 toward baseline 3 (cap 4 if none were sold —
  scarcity premium). Tuna +3 toward 8 (cap 10 if none sold).
- **Fish breed:** each spot with **2+ fish left** gains **+2 fish** (add 1 Cod +
  1 of its rarer species, up to its starting stock). A spot with **≤1 fish left
  gains nothing — it's collapsed.**
- **Sea Health check:** count all fish tokens left in all cups. Starting total is
  ~46. If it ever ends a day **below 18 (~40%)**, the sea is *in crisis*; if it's
  **still below 18 at game end, every player's cash is halved.** Nobody wins on a
  dead sea.

### End of Day 5
Most cash wins — unless the sea collapsed, in which case everyone's halved and
you all half-lost together. That's the semi-coop bite.

## Layer 2 — add these *only after* the core feels good

Introduce one at a time so you can tell what each adds:

- **Upgrades** (buy at dock, one per day): Bigger Hold (+2 cap, cost 6) · Fish
  Finder (peek in one cup before Set Sail, cost 5) · Fast Boat (win all spot ties
  + reach any spot, cost 6). *Tests the compounding "invest vs. cash in" loop.*
- **Cannery** (cost 7): once per day, sell 2 Cod as if 1 Bass. *Tests
  demand-shaping — turning the safe staple into the volatile money-maker.*
- **AI "chaos" seat:** deal a face-up "Rival Skipper" whose spot and sales you
  resolve by a simple rule card (e.g. "always fishes the richest known spot, always
  dumps its whole hold"). *Tests whether an AI can monkey the market believably.*

---

## Tuning knobs (if something feels off)

- **Market too swingy / not swingy enough?** Change the per-sale price drop.
- **Timing feels punishing?** Move the snap to tension 4, or fewer Snags in cups.
- **Sea never/always collapses?** Move the 18 threshold, or the +2 breed rate.
- **Games too short/long?** 4 Days ≈ shorter, 6 ≈ longer.
- **Runaway leader?** Lean on the Inspector forecast and rubber-band events.

Write down what you changed and what happened — that log *is* the game design.

```
DAY: 1 Forecast  2 Set Sail (secret, reveal)  3 Cast & Reel  4 Market  5 Overnight
REEL: draw 1 at a time to hold(5). Snag=+1 tension. Stop anytime.
      tension 3 = SNAP (lose best fish, end turn).
SELL: Cod=1 flat. Bass -1/sale (min1). Tuna -2/sale (min3).
NIGHT: unsold spoil (-1 each). Bass +2→3(cap4). Tuna +3→8(cap10).
       spot w/ 2+ fish: +2. spot w/ ≤1: dead.
SEA HEALTH < 18 at end = everyone halved. 5 DAYS. Most cash wins.
```

## After the playtest — the road ahead

This kit is the gate. Depending on how it plays:

1. **If the core sings** → I write a **v0.2** with the winning Layer-2 modules,
   then we scope the **digital greybox**: the real question there is the
   **TV-hub + iPad** tech (a web app with room-code join is almost certainly the
   right call — no App Store, any tablet, cheap to build). We build the *ugly*
   version of the loop first — no art — and confirm it's fun with the timers and
   the live market on screen.
2. **If a piece is off** → we tune it on paper (free) until it clicks, then
   greybox.
3. **In parallel, low-stakes:** art/tone identity for Overboard (the "not cute,
   not 8-bit, characterful" look), the cast of rival skippers, and a name we
   actually love.

**Decisions still open** (no rush — the playtest will inform most): final player
count, how spicy the commons/rubber-band gets, art direction, and the TV+iPad
tech stack. We'll take them one at a time.
