# Macatuda — Family Game Project

A local-multiplayer family game in the spirit of **M.U.L.E.**: 15–30 minutes,
easy to learn, secretly deep, character-driven, and different every time.
Played on a shared **TV hub** with each player using an **iPad as their private
controller** — the way Jackbox and Mario Party split public drama from private
decisions.

## Where we are

**Phase 1 — Concept exploration.** We're brainstorming the game before writing
any code (or even any rules). The current thinking lives in:

- [`docs/concept-exploration.md`](docs/concept-exploration.md) — design pillars,
  the flagship concept with a full round walkthrough, alternate concepts,
  a toolbox of mechanics worth stealing from M.U.L.E., the AI "chaos player"
  cast, and open questions to decide as a family.

## The one-line pitch

> A band of critter homesteaders settle a new valley every game. Claim land,
> put goofy helpers to work, survive weird weather, and haggle in a live market
> — win the most for your family without letting the whole valley go bust.

Nothing here is locked. Phase 1 is for finding the fun.
